A short history of the lighthouse

Lighthouses guided ships along dangerous coasts for more than two thousand years. The first towers burned wood fires, and later ones used oil lamps and lenses that could throw a beam for many miles.

Keepers lived at the base of the tower and climbed the stairs every evening to light the lamp. Today most lights are automatic, and the keepers' houses are museums. Read more about keepers.

The tallest lighthouse in the world stands in the harbour of Jeddah and is about 133 metres high.
